# Welcome to Selfplace

We're thrilled to introduce **Selfplace** — a calm, emotionally intelligent mobile application designed to help you reconnect with yourself through daily reflections, mood check-ins, and gentle guidance.

## What is Selfplace?

Selfplace is not a productivity tool. It's not a habit tracker. It's a **quiet companion** — a space where you can show up exactly as you are, check in with how you're feeling, and receive thoughtful insights tailored to your emotional journey.

## What's inside?

- **Daily Check-ins** — Track your emotional state with gentle prompts and mood cards
- **AI Insights** — Receive personalized summaries of your emotional patterns  
- **Astrology & Energy** — Explore cosmic rhythms tied to your zodiac sign
- **Guidance Cards** — Draw from a deck of interactive emotional guidance cards
- **Living Mascot** — A gentle companion that adapts to your mood

## Available Now

Selfplace is available on the **App Store**. Download it today and begin your inner journey.
